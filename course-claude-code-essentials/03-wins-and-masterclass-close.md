# What Actually Gets Faster

- Ramp time on unfamiliar modules (explain + doc before you thrash)
- Small, well-scoped changes with tests
- Mechanical refactors when the brief is tight and the diff is reviewed
- Not: skipping design, review or accountability

# Before versus After Today

### Before
- Hours to orient in a dark corner of the repo
- Blank-page feature anxiety
- Inconsistent review depth under time pressure

### After
- Plan-mode orientation briefs
- Narrow change plans you can initial
- Diff-first merge habits

# Napkin ROI (Keep It Conservative)

- One onboarding doc session saves the next human an hour
- Two small ticketed changes/week × 20 minutes saved = real calendar return
- One prevented secret leak or auth bug is worth more than a month of autocomplete
- Measure after three sober tasks—not after the classroom dopamine
